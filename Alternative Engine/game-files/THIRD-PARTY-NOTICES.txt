Forge card scripts and skin assets
----------------------------------
The per-card rules scripts, set and format lists under runtime/forge are taken
unmodified from Forge and are licensed under the GNU General Public License
version 3. Mana symbols, keyword ability icons and the optional sleeves and
table backgrounds also come from Forge's default skin. The matching Forge
revision is recorded in runtime/BUILD-PROVENANCE.txt (ForgeCommit); the
corresponding source is that revision of the Forge repository. A copy of the
licence is included at runtime/licenses/Forge-GPL-3.0.txt.

Solo Arcanum does not contain or run the Forge program. Games run on Solo
Arcanum's own rules engine, which reads these scripts as data.

Forge source: https://github.com/Card-Forge/forge
